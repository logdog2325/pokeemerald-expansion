# Draconid Emerald – story outline and build status

Every story event in play order, marked by how far it is built. Kept up to date as work lands; the detailed
source of truth is [hack_story.md](hack_story.md) (the playtester's notes), the scenes and their dialogue are in
[hack_script.md](hack_script.md), the reasons in [hack_decisions.md](hack_decisions.md).

Legend: ✅ built, tested and pushed · ☑️ built and merged, in its last test round · 🔨 built on its own branch,
being merged · 🚧 being built now · ⬜ not built yet (planned)

## Act 1 – The prophecy (Draconid Village → Rustboro)
1. ✅ Night prologue: a falling star; the player wakes at home, Aster at the door – the Elder is calling.
2. ✅ Wall clock; the Elder's house: the meteor prophecy and the mission (walk among Team Magma, stand beside
   Rayquaza when the sky splits).
3. ✅ The shrine: the egg ceremony – Deino (Unova), Dreepy (Galar), Jangmo-o (Alola); the player chooses, the
   Elder keeps one aside (Nerine's), Aster takes the leftover.
4. ✅ The egg hatches after 5 steps outside; a villager gives the Running Shoes.
5. ✅ Draconid Pass: Aster's tutorial battle.
6. ✅ Route 101 → Littleroot: the Poochyena rescue; Birch's family (Brendan is Birch's son) and Norman's (May is
   Norman's daughter); Birch's lab – Brendan gets Treecko, May Torchic, the player the Pokédex (National Dex).
7. ✅ Route 103: May's battle.
8. ✅ Petalburg Woods: Nerine (in Aqua disguise) fight 1; Courtney recruits the player; the outpost cabin; the
   Team Magma uniform (reputation: "uniform" from here).
9. ✅ Rustboro: Brendan confronts the Magma recruit (Treecko, Poochyena, Taillow, Slakoth).
10. ✅ Stone Badge → Tabitha's orders; Prof. Oak outside the Gym gives the second partner (Charmander,
    Totodile or Treecko), puzzled by the uniform.

## Act 2 – Deep cover (Devon Goods → Slateport → Mauville)
11. ✅ The Devon Goods theft (Nerine) and Tabitha's order; Rusturf Tunnel: Nerine fight 2, the goods choice
    (return them to Devon or keep them for Magma).
12. ✅ Mr. Stone: the PokéNav and the Gen 6 Exp. Share.
13. ✅ Route 104: Brendan comes out of Mr. Briney's cottage and battles the player.
14. ✅ Mr. Briney's boat: Maxie's first PokéNav call (his calls guide the player until the reveal).
15. ✅ Dewford (Brawly), Granite Cave (Steven, the letter).
16. ✅ Slateport, Oceanic Museum: Tabitha's order, Nerine fight 3.
17. ✅ Route 110: May's battle; she registers the player to "keep tabs on MAGMA".
18. ✅ Mauville: Wally's hostile battle; Wattson.

## Act 3 – Rising in the ranks (Meteor Falls → Lavaridge)
19. ✅ Meteor Falls: Maxie in person, the meteorite shard, the stand-off with Aqua; Aster's battle (losable).
20. ✅ Route 112: Wally's battle below the cable car.
21. ✅ Mt. Chimney: Tabitha's order, Nerine fight 4, Brendan's battle, the player secretly pulls the meteorite
    and the machine dies.
22. ✅ Jagged Pass: Aster gives the Mega Ring.
23. ✅ Lavaridge: a Draconid traveller gives the second partner's Mega Stone; May battles, then hands over the
    Go-Goggles; Flannery.

## Act 4 – The orbs (Petalburg → Weather Institute → Fortree → Mt. Pyre → Magma Hideout)
24. ✅ Petalburg Gym: Wally at the door; Norman's battle while May watches.
25. ✅ Route 119: Tabitha's order; Weather Institute (Shelly); May covers for the player.
26. ✅ Route 119: Brendan's battle, HM Fly, the PokéNav registration.
27. ✅ Route 120: Steven and the Devon Scope; Wally's battle on the bridge. Fortree (Winona).
28. ✅ Lilycove: Wally (Mega Gallade); Brendan + May double battle.
29. ✅ Mt. Pyre: Maxie's order and the Magma Emblem; Nerine fight 5; Archie takes the Red Orb.
30. ✅ Jagged Pass guard → Magma Hideout: Maxie's promotion ("my right hand"), no battle; on the way out,
    Brendan's battle.

## Act 5 – The betrayal (Aqua Hideout → Mossdeep → Seafloor → Sootopolis)
31. ✅ Aqua Hideout (Maxie's order): Nerine fight 6 in disguise; Archie leaves in the submarine.
32. ✅ Mossdeep (Tate & Liza); the Space Center raid: the player + Tabitha vs Steven + Brendan; Steven: "I know
    you let us win"; Brendan storms off. May's battle outside (Mega Blaziken).
33. ✅ Seafloor Cavern: Nerine drops her disguise – fight 7 with her Mega; Kyogre wakes. Route 128: Maxie and
    Archie.
34. ✅ Sootopolis – the turn: the player refuses Maxie and drops the uniform (reputation: "revealed"); Maxie solo,
    then Maxie + Archie multi battle with Brendan or May as partner.
    🚧 Being rewritten as a big scene: Maxie pieces the lies together in front of everyone, Tabitha takes it
    personally, Maxie swears revenge.
35. ✅ Rayquaza: Aster and Nerine's Draconid calling; Rayquaza calms the titans; the Elder's call; the rivals
    make up; Wallace gives Waterfall; Juan.

## Act 5½ – Team Magma's revenge (Sootopolis → Victory Road)  🚧 being built now
36. 🚧 Magma grunt ambushes leaving Sootopolis and on the sea routes.
37. 🚧 Maxie battle #2 (Mega Camerupt); a parting line about the player's home.
38. 🚧 The Aqua gauntlet on the way to Victory Road: 5–6 grunts back to back with no healing, then Shelly
    (Mega Gyarados).
39. 🚧 Victory Road: Courtney (Mega Houndoom) + Tabitha (fire Mega) double battle; they hint that Maxie and
    Archie will make the Draconids pay.

## Act 6 – Champion (Victory Road → Pokémon League)  🔨 built on its branch, being merged
40. 🔨 Victory Road: Wally (Mega Gallade).
41. 🔨 The Elite Four (ORAS rosters) and Champion Steven (his ORAS team, Mega Metagross); Brendan and May reach the
    Champion's room.
42. 🔨 Hall of Fame without credits → home; the meteor on TV; the Elder's summons (his dragon flies the player).

## Act 7 – The prophecy fulfilled
43. ⬜ The Elder calls the player home first: the shrine's sealed wall opens – Regidrago.
44. 🔨 Sky Pillar: the trial of three (the player + Nerine vs Aster); Zinnia on 3F; the summit – catch Rayquaza,
    Dragon Ascent, the Deoxys boss, Mega Rayquaza destroys the meteor.
45. ⬜ The alarm: Maxie and Archie used the Orbs – Primal Groudon and Primal Kyogre – and are attacking the
    Draconid village.
46. ⬜ The village under attack: Brendan, May, Wally and Aster fight at the player's side – four double
    battles, one with each as partner, against Magma + Aqua grunts; Brendan and May get their Latis.
47. ⬜ The final battle at the shrine: the player + Brendan or May (Mega Lati) vs Maxie (Primal Groudon) and
    Archie (Primal Kyogre).
48. ⬜ Aftermath: the titans calm and go home; Maxie beaten but bitter, Archie near regret; the Orbs; the
    village rebuilds; credits.

## Post-game
49. 🔨 The Elder brings the S.S. Ticket; the Lati news.  ⬜ Both Latis roam Hoenn.
50. 🔨 Brendan and May in the lab (singles and a double); Aster at the shrine; Nerine by the village pond;
    Deoxys on the Sky Pillar summit.
51. ✅ The Elite Four and Champion Steven rematch with their ORAS post-game teams; after the finale Wallace battles by
    the Cave of Origin; Steven chats at Meteor Falls.
52. ✅ Battle Frontier: Wes, Red and Blue; the Legends' Tag (team with a beaten legend against the other two).
53. ⬜ Groudon (Magma Hideout) and Kyogre (Seafloor Cavern) catchable.

## Systems
- ✅ Reputation (pre-uniform / uniform / revealed) for NPCs, shops, gyms; the tamer ↔ Magma outfit.
- ✅ Maxie's 10 PokéNav calls (they stop at the reveal); the rivals' PokéNav calls follow their story and the
  player's reputation.
- ✅ Hard level caps; rival teams grow through the story; Latis kept off the rivals until the finale.
- ✅ Gen 6 Exp. Share; HM field moves without a Pokémon that knows them; no trade evolutions; the egg dragons
  evolve at 25 and 50.
- ✅ Gen 4–9 Pokémon in the wild (Beldum 1% in Granite Cave), National Dex from the start.
  ☑️ Gen 4–9 Pokémon on ~60% of generic trainers, every gym trainer and the grunts.
- ✅ The battle-item counter in every Mart (stock grows with badges), Gym Leaders' boosters, Mega Stones through
  the story after the Mega Ring.
- ✅ Story-lock checker for Acts 1–5.  ⬜ Acts 6–7 and the post-game.
- ⬜ Final phase: hard-lock sweep, base-Emerald contradiction scrub, per-act debug jumps + playtest guide, the
  v2 test ROM.
