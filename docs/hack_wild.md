# Draconid Emerald – wild Pokémon (Gens 1–9, the Draconid Pass, the 1% Beldum)

Round 1, feedback 1.28: *"add some wild pokemon from gens 4 5 6 7 8 9 where it makes sense and also to random
trainers roster and have there be a 1% chance to encounter beldum in granite cave"*. Decisions D-193 – D-197
([hack_decisions.md](hack_decisions.md)); the trainer half is in [hack_trainers.md](hack_trainers.md#gen-49-swaps-round-1).

Round 2, feedback 2.2 – 2.4: *"also I think there are no pokemon you can encounter in draconid pass you probably
want to make an encounter table for that area"*, *"also did you add some gens 1-9 pokemon on every route that makes
sense for the area?"* and, on Larvesta, *"also make it a 1% encounter in petalburg too"*. Decisions D-300 – D-302:
a table for the [Draconid Pass](#draconid-pass-d-300), the Kanto and Johto species the Hoenn Pokédex leaves out
(Gens 1–2: every Gen 3 species is in it), more variety on the seas, and Larvesta at 1% in Petalburg Woods.

The data is `src/data/wild_encounters.json` (Hoenn tables and the Draconid Pass; the FRLG, Battle Pyramid and
Battle Pike tables are untouched). `python3 tools/hack/check_wild.py` checks it; the table at the end of this page
is generated by `python3 tools/hack/check_wild.py --doc`.

## Rules (D-193, D-197, D-301)

- **Only species, never levels.** A new species takes an existing slot and keeps that slot's vanilla levels, so
  every table keeps its level range and Emerald's fixed slot rates (land 20/20/10/10/10/10/5/5/4/4/1/1, surf and
  Rock Smash 60/30/5/4/1, Old Rod 70/30, Good Rod 60/20/20, Super Rod 40/40/15/4/1). The slot's rate is the new
  species' rarity: common newcomers take a 10% land slot, uncommon ones 5%/4%, rare ones (dragons, Larvesta,
  Mimikyu, Elekid, Kangaskhan, Scyther, the Beldum) a 1% slot. Only a map the hack added gets a whole new table
  (the Draconid Pass, D-300).
- **Only duplicate slots.** A new species replaces a slot whose species has another slot in the same table, so
  **no table loses a species** and every vanilla Hoenn species is still wild exactly where it was (no species was
  moved). The newcomers' share of a land table stays **at most ~30%** (now 9–26%: Mt. Pyre's summit the least,
  Granite Cave B1F the most); a surf table keeps its first (60%) slot vanilla. 361 slots in 110 tables (281 from
  round 1, 80 from round 2); every route has 3–6 species its vanilla table doesn't have, the seas 3–4 (round 1 left
  most of them at 2). The Safari Zone's Johto areas, Artisan Cave, Altering Cave, Mirage Island and the Sootopolis
  crater keep their vanilla tables.
- **Habitat, region, story point.** Forest routes get bugs and birds, caves rock / ground / ghost / bats, the
  desert ground types, volcanic ground fire types, the seas water types; Hoenn is subtropical, so Alolan and
  Paldean species fit it well. Early areas get first stages (Skwovet, Starly, Kricketot, Nymble, Sentret, Ledyba);
  later areas the stage the slot's level allows (Luxio and Furret on Route 118, Staravia on Route 115, Trumbeak on
  Route 119, Haunter at Mt. Pyre's summit, Boldore, Gabite, Ursaring and Steelix on Victory Road).
- **Gens 1–2 by their home games (D-301).** The Kanto and Johto species go where their own games put them, never
  everywhere (one habitat each, mostly one area; a sea species along one stretch of sea): Mt. Moon's Clefairy in
  Meteor Falls (the meteor cave), Diglett in the tunnel being dug, Onix and Dunsparce in Granite Cave, the Pokémon
  Tower's Gastly, Haunter and Cubone (and Johto's Misdreavus) on Mt. Pyre, Seafoam's Seel and Shellder and the Ice
  Path's Swinub in Shoal Cave, Kanto Route 4's Ekans on Route 116 (the way to Rusturf, like Route 4 to Mt. Moon),
  the fire horse, dogs and Magby around Mt. Chimney, the Kanto Safari Zone's Nidoran, Tauros, Kangaskhan and
  Scyther in the Hoenn Safari Zone, Slowpoke on the lazy beaches, Krabby and Shellder on the Good Rod, Mantine on
  the open western seas and Remoraid on the eastern ones (where Mantyke lives, so Mantyke can evolve), Qwilfish on
  the southern seas. Psyduck, Horsea, Staryu, Chinchou and Phanpy are Kanto / Johto species too, but in the Hoenn
  Pokédex and already wild in vanilla Emerald, so they stay where they were.
- **What may appear.** Species enabled in this build with a front pic, a cry and a level-up learnset; no
  legendary, sub-legendary, mythical, paradox or Ultra Beast; no Mega / Gigantamax / Totem form. **No regional
  forms**: every Hoenn species keeps its Hoenn look (a Galarian Zigzagoon next to the Hoenn one would read as a
  bug). The Deino, Dreepy and Jangmo-o lines stay out of the wild (the three Draconid eggs, D-065); other dragons
  are rare and late: Gible 1% in the desert, Goomy 1% in the rain of Routes 119/120 (5% on 120), Noibat and
  Druddigon in the Draconid country of Meteor Falls, Druddigon 1% at the top of the Sky Pillar.
- **Forms**: Minior is its Meteor Form (red core; the core colour only shows under Shields Down), Basculin
  red-striped, Deerling spring, Sinistea phony, Wishiwashi solo, Mimikyu disguised, Flabébé red flower – each the
  species' default form.
- **Levels** (D-197): `check_wild.py` maps every area to the story segment in which the player first reaches it
  (`WILD_SEGMENTS`, round 1 order; Surf S6, Dive S8, Rock Smash S4, Old / Good / Super Rod S2 / S3 / S7 can make a
  table later). A slot the hack changes must keep its vanilla levels or stay at most **3 levels** over that
  segment's cap (`LEVEL_MARGIN`). Vanilla levels are kept even where they are higher (Route 115's Lv 23–26 land
  table in S2, Good Rod slots up to Lv 30 in S3): wild Pokémon are caught, and the hard cap stops them levelling
  until the next badge. A new species is never below the level at which its line evolves by level-up.

## Beldum at 1% in Granite Cave (D-194)

Every Granite Cave land table has Beldum in one of its two 1% slots, at a level of that floor:

| Map | Slot | Was | Beldum |
|---|---|---|---|
| Granite Cave 1F | 11 (1%) | Geodude Lv 9 (Geodude keeps slots 8–10) | Lv 9 |
| Granite Cave B1F | 11 (1%) | Sableye Lv 11 (Sableye keeps slots 8–10) | Lv 11 |
| Granite Cave B2F | 10 (1%) | Sableye Lv 12 (Sableye keeps slots 7, 8, 11; Carbink took slot 9, Onix slot 6) | Lv 12 |
| Steven's Room | 11 (1%) | Aron Lv 8 (Aron keeps slots 8, 10; Carbink took slot 9) | Lv 8 |

Beldum's catch rate is 3, so it is a real prize at S2. `check_wild.py` fails if any Granite Cave land table has no
Beldum, or has it outside a 1% slot or the floor's level range.

## Draconid Pass (D-300)

The short mountain path from Draconid Village (the brown Fallarbor highland, north) over the stream (rows 14–17, the
bridge) into a green valley that opens east onto Route 101 had no table, so its tall grass (metatile 0x00D: x 4–9,
y 19–23; x 5–9, y 28–31; x 15–21, y 29–32) gave nothing. The player walks it at the very start (S1, the Lv 5
hatchling, Aster's Lv 5 battle at the bridge), so the land table is **Lv 3–5**, between the pass's battle and Route
101's Lv 2–3, with Emerald's rates (land 20, surf 4, fishing 30, as on Route 102):

- **Land**: the Hoenn regulars of Routes 101–104 (Zigzagoon, Poochyena 20% each, Wurmple, Taillow) take 69%; the
  newcomers 31% – Pidgey and Bidoof (10%, a highland bird and the stream's beaver), Teddiursa (5%, the bear cub of
  Blackthorn's Route 45, the Johto dragon clan's mountains), Mareep (4%, a highland pasture), Rockruff and Flabébé
  (1%, the valley's loyal pup and its flowers). No dragon (D-195: the Deino / Dreepy / Jangmo-o lines never wild,
  other dragons rare and late), only first stages.
- **Surf** (S6) and **fishing** (Old / Good / Super Rod S2 / S3 / S7): a cold mountain stream like Route 102's pond –
  Marill, Wooper and Quagsire, the Poliwag line, Goldeen and Seaking, Basculin (Super Rod 4%), Magikarp. Its levels
  follow Route 102's, except the Good Rod: 10–25 instead of 10–30, since a new table has no vanilla levels to keep
  and must stay within the S3 cap + 3 (28).

`check_wild.py` knows the map as hack-only (`HACK_MAPS`): no vanilla counterpart, the field's slot count and a rate
some vanilla Hoenn table uses, every level within the area's cap + 3; any other table without a vanilla counterpart
is an error. `tests/draconid_pass.play` (after `opening.play`, from `opening_after_aster.ss`) walks into the
valley's grass until five wild battles have started and checks each species against the table (`expect_wild`).
The full table is at the top of [Table changes](#table-changes).

Feedback 2.4 (*"also is larvesta in petalburg forrest?"* – *"also make it a 1% encounter in petalburg too"*):

## Pokédex: National from the start (D-196)

Most new species are not in the Hoenn Pokédex (nor is the player's dragon), and Hoenn mode lists only Hoenn
numbers. The Pokédex therefore starts in **National mode**: Birch's lab scene (`data/scripts/draconid/birch_intro.pory`)
calls `special EnableNationalPokedex` right after `LittlerootTown_ProfessorBirchsLab_EventScript_ReceivePokedex`,
and Birch adds a line that Pokémon from faraway regions turn up in Hoenn now. The Hoenn mode is still in the
Pokédex menu (the Hoenn count, Birch's Hoenn rating and the diploma are unchanged). `TODO(dialogue)`: the post-game
National Dex scene in Birch's lab still calls it an upgrade – reword it with the post-game rework.

## Generic trainers

122 generic trainers (28% of the 434 first battles, 214 blocks with their rematch tiers) swap one Pokémon (two for
Timothy, Nicolas and Wilton) for a Gen 4–9 species of their class and area; see
[hack_trainers.md](hack_trainers.md#gen-49-swaps-round-1) (D-195). The second pass (feedback 1.40, D-240 – D-242)
brings it to 269 generic trainers (62%), two in teams of 4+ from S5 on, a Gen 4–9 Pokémon of the gym's type for
every gym trainer, and one for 44 of the 49 grunts.
