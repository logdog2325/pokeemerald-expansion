# Draconid Emerald – playtest guide (v2)

What to play, what to look for, and how to report it.
This is the v2 story: round 1 and round 2.
Write findings in [hack_feedback.md](hack_feedback.md).

## The two ROMs

| ROM | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `pokeemerald-release.gba` | a real playthrough (no debug menu) |
| `pokeemerald.gba` (debug) | jumping around: hold **R**, press **START** |

Any GBA emulator works (mGBA is best).
In-game saves carry over between builds.
Emulator savestates don't.

## What's different from Emerald

- **The opening.** The Elder of the Draconid clan gives the new-game speech, with his old Salamence.
  That night a falling star crosses the sky over the village.
- **Your home** is the Draconid village in the mountains west of Route 101. No Mom, no truck.
- **Your egg.** In the shrine the Elder offers three eggs: Deino, Dreepy or Jangmo-o.
  It hatches after 5 steps outside. Aster, his apprentice, takes another one.
- **Birch and the meteor.** On Route 101 Birch kneels by last night's star.
  A dazed Lunatone fell with it. Your hatchling calms it down.
- **Joining Team Magma.** In Petalburg Woods, Courtney sees you beat an "Aqua grunt".
  She asks you to join: YES or NO (NO only makes her laugh: you go with her anyway).
  You get the red uniform in a cabin at the edge of the woods.
- **The uniform.** From then on almost everyone reacts to it: cold, scared, angry, whispering.
  After the turn at Sootopolis the same people are sorry, or grateful.
- **Nerine** is a Draconid hiding in Team Aqua. She battles you all through the story.
  She drops hints. Her disguise comes off in the Seafloor Cavern.
- **The Devon Goods.** In Rusturf Tunnel you choose: give them back to Devon, or keep them for Magma.
  People remember which. Kept: Tabitha pays you. Returned: Mr. Stone's Amulet Coin.
- **Maxie calls you** on the PokéNav after each mission and names the next place.
  He stops once you turn on him.
- **Your rivals are against you.** Brendan and May think you really joined Magma.
  So does Wally, and he is angry about it. They fight you again and again.
- **Prof. Oak** gives a second partner after the first badge: Charmander, Totodile or Treecko.
- **Megas and Z-Moves.** The Z-Power Ring comes with your egg.
  Z-Crystals turn up along the way: Prof. Oak, Aster, Nerine, item balls.
  Aster brings the Mega Ring in Act 3. A Draconid traveller gives your partner's Mega Stone.
  More stones lie around Hoenn and in the shops' battle item counters.
  After the Champion the counter sells every Mega Stone and every Z-Crystal.
- **The turn at Sootopolis.** You take off the uniform in front of Maxie and fight him.
  Then you and Brendan or May take on Maxie and Archie together.
- **Team Magma's revenge.** On the way to the League, Magma and Aqua ambush you again and again.
- **Steven is the Champion.** Wallace is the Sootopolis Gym's former leader and waits later.
- **Drake** of the Elite Four is the Elder's younger brother. He left the clan forty years ago.
- **Act 7.** No credits after the Hall of Fame: a meteor is coming.
  The Elder calls you home, Regidrago wakes under the shrine, then the Sky Pillar, Rayquaza and Deoxys.
  Then Magma and Aqua attack the village, with Primal Groudon and Kyogre.
- **The post-game.** Lance flies in to the village. Wes, Red and Blue wait at the Battle Frontier.
  The Elite Four, Steven and your rivals have rematch teams. Groudon, Kyogre, Deoxys and both Latis can be caught.
- **Level caps** stop EXP at each badge's cap: 15, 20, 25, 30, 34, 38, 44, 48, then 60 until you're Champion.

**Losing a battle**: "whiteout" means you wake at the last Pokémon Center and the scene waits for you.
"Goes on" means you are healed and the story goes on (or you get to try again).

## Jump to an act (debug ROM)

Start any game on the debug ROM (a new one is fine, even in the bedroom).
In the overworld, hold **R** and press **START**.
**Jump to act…** is the first entry. Press **A**, pick a stop, press **A**.

You land a few steps from the act's first scene. Walk on and it starts.
The game is set up as a real playthrough would be at that point:
badges, key items, HMs, story progress, beaten trainers, Fly spots, your outfit.

Your own Pokémon stay. If they are behind the level cap there,
your dragon and Prof. Oak's partner join, evolved, at the cap.
You also get money, potions, Revives and Poké Balls.

Your choices are kept: your egg, Oak's partner, the Devon Goods, Regidrago.
If you haven't made one yet, the jump picks: Deino, Charmander, goods returned, Regidrago not caught.

Going back to an earlier act asks **"This rewinds the story. Continue?"**
NO leaves everything as it was. YES rewinds: later badges and items go.

| Stop | You land | Set up | First scene |
|---|---|---|---|
| ACT 1: P. WOODS | Petalburg Woods, south of the researcher | the Pokédex, no badge, tamer outfit | two steps north: Nerine's robbery, then Courtney |
| ACT 2: RUSTBORO | outside the Rustboro Gym | Stone Badge, Oak's partner, Magma uniform | three steps west: Nerine steals the goods |
| ACT 3: METEOR FALLS | Meteor Falls, east of the bridge | 3 badges, Rock Smash, a bike, Oak's crystals | three steps west: Maxie takes the meteorite |
| ACT 4: PETALBURG | Petalburg, south of the Gym | 4 badges, Strength, Mega Ring, your partner's Mega Stone | two steps north: Wally at the Gym door |
| ACT 5: AQUA HIDEOUT | the hideout's submarine dock | 6 badges, Surf, Fly, promoted | talk to Matt: Nerine steps in |
| MAGMA'S REVENGE | in front of Juan's Gym door | 8 badges, Dive, Waterfall, tamer outfit again | one step off: the ambush |
| ACT 6: THE LEAGUE | the League's lobby | the revenge battles done | talk to the door guards |
| ACT 7: ELDER'S CALL | the Sky Pillar's foot | Champion, Hall of Fame done | the Elder's call at once |
| ACT 7: THE VILLAGE | the village's south end | the meteor broken, Rayquaza with Dragon Ascent | the attack at once |
| POST-GAME | downstairs at home | SS Ticket, both Latis roaming | out of the door: the post-game village |

From Act 7 on there is no level cap; the jump uses Lv 60, 65 and 72.
A jump saves nothing by itself: save the game when you want to keep it.

## Things worth pushing on

- **Difficulty.** Is each gym leader and ace beatable at the cap without grinding?
  Note the fight, your team and levels.
- **The uniform.** Talk to everyone in uniform, then again after Sootopolis.
- **Both genders.** Your own sprites everywhere: running, biking, surfing, the trainer card, the Hall of Fame.
  Brendan and May never stand in for you.
- **Sequence breaks.** Anything you can skip, anyone still standing after their scene, a scene that plays twice.
- **Text.** Lines that still assume you moved to Littleroot, or that call someone by the wrong name.

## Known placeholders (don't report)

- The opening movie and the credits' bike scenes still show Brendan and May.
- The shrine's Rayquaza statue and the meteorites use vanilla graphics.
- Mega Rayquaza flies off as the normal Rayquaza sprite.
