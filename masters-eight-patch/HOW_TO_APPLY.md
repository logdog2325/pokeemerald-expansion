# Masters Eight (v3.1.0) for Pokemon Battle Theater

On your PC, in your `pokemon-battle-theater` folder (on v3.0.0):

    git am 0001-v3.1.0-Masters-Eight-the-Journeys-World-Coronation-S.patch
    make

Then make your .bps from the new pokeemerald.gba as usual. Your local LiYun
backgrounds / music are used as normal (they aren't in the patch).
